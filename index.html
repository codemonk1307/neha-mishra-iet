01 · About

An artist disguised as a software engineer

Five years of shipping systems that move money and data at scale, and an equal number spent behind an easel and a microphone.

I write software at Salesforce, where I work on the platform side of a CRM that millions of people open every morning. Before that I spent three years at Arcesium on Trade Reference Data (transaction flows, receivables reconciliation and the unglamorous reporting pipelines that hedge funds quietly depend on), then a year at Barclays architecting AWS infrastructure.

Away from the terminal I paint, I do stand-up, and I read obsessively in two languages. I have never found these to be separate skills. Debugging and punchlines are the same craft: notice the thing everyone else walked past, then say it precisely.
